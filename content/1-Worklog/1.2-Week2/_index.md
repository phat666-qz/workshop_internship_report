### Week 2 Objectives:

* Delve into AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) service.
* Distinguish and effectively apply core IAM components including Users, Groups, Roles, and Policies.
* Implement the Principle of Least Privilege to ensure the security of the AWS account.
* Research advanced account security methods such as Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and AWS Security Token Service (STS).
* Survey concepts around Cost Management, Billing, and AWS Free Tier limits.
* Practically configure AWS Budgets to proactively control and alert on incurred costs.
